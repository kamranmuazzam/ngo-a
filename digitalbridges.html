What we do

We strengthen communities through education by supporting underserved schools with innovative pedagogy and access to technology

The CLEC Model

A Collaborative Learning Environment Center (CLEC) is our signature approach. Purpose-built rooms where technology, smart design, and hands-on teaching come together. We co-design each CLEC with local schools and communities, so it actually fits the people using it.

Tech Access

CLEC ensures equitable access to technology by providing laptops, internet connectivity, and smart boards—bridging the gap so rural schools can offer learning experiences comparable to urban classrooms.

Teacher Training

We provide structured and need-based training programs for teachers and community education leaders. The training focuses on strengthening pedagogical skills, digital literacy, and classroom innovation to improve learning outcomes.

University Credit

Through the Kathmandu University credit course system, teachers receive recognized professional development opportunities that contribute to their continuous learning and career growth. The training is practical, hands-on, and tailored to real classroom needs, ensuring meaningful impact on teaching quality and community education leadership.

Donor's journey

Every year, DB4N brings a small group of donors on an exclusive guided trip to Nepal. You’ll visit the schools and community centers where CLECs are operating, meet the students and teachers, share meals, and experience Nepali culture in places most visitors never reach.  Spots are limited. This is for people who want to go beyond writing a check.

NOT A LAB – A LEARNING HUB
CLASSROOM
The Collaborative Learning Environment Center (CLEC) strengthens school-based education by integrating digital infrastructure, interactive learning tools, and teacher support systems. It transforms classrooms into engaging, technology-enabled spaces that enhance learning outcomes and equip students with essential 21st-century skills.
COMMUNITY

Beyond schools, CLEC functions as an inclusive community learning and training hub, providing youth women’s groups  and communities with access to digital literacy, skills development, and livelihood-oriented training. It fosters local capacity building, empowerment, and equitable access to learning opportunities in  rural and underserved areas.

STUDENT LEARNING JOURNEY

At the Collaborative Learning Environment Centers (CLECs), students across Nepal are gaining essential digital skills by using tools like Microsoft Excel and Word for practical applications, including designing billing templates, managing data, and creating professional certificates. Through hands-on collaboration and smart board presentations, students not only build technical proficiency but also gain the confidence and self-reliance needed to thrive in a technology-driven future.
